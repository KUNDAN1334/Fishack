The webhook failures following your upgrade to v2.3 were caused by your receiving endpoint exceeding our strict response time limit. Flowlytics requires endpoints to acknowledge receipt within 10s. When your server took longer than this threshold to respond, the delivery attempt timed out. Flowlytics automatically marks any slow endpoints as failed and subsequently triggers our retry mechanism, resulting in the duplicate and delayed event processing you observed.

To resolve this issue, update your receiving endpoint to return a 200 OK status immediately upon receiving the payload, before running any heavy database queries or business logic. You must then process the event data asynchronously in the background.
