The SCIM deprovisioning issue was caused by the SCIM connector being scoped to a group that did not include the user. This resulted in the SCIM connector not being able to identify the user for deprovisioning. To resolve this issue, we updated the SCIM connector to be scoped to the correct group that includes the user. This ensures that the SCIM connector can properly identify the user and remove their access to Flowlytics. The correct group was identified by verifying the user's membership in the system. By making this change, the SCIM deprovisioning process was able to successfully remove the user's access to Flowlytics.
