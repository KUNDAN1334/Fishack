The ERR_TIMEOUT_502 errors you experienced during webhook deliveries were caused by your load balancer idle timeout being configured below the actual time required to process the delivery payload. Because the receiving server took longer to respond than the gateway limit permitted, the connection was terminated prematurely, resulting in the 502 status code. 

To resolve this issue, you need to increase your load balancer idle timeout to at least 30 seconds. This adjustment ensures that long-running webhook requests have sufficient time to complete successfully without interruption. Once updated, you can trigger a manual retry for the failed deliveries from your Flowlytics dashboard.
