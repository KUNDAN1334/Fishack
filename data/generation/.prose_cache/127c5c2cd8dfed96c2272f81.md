Under the terms of the Globex billing agreement (version 1.0), Globex receives exactly one consolidated invoice per quarter for all platform usage and associated services managed through Flowlytics. This consolidated billing schedule replaces individual monthly statements to streamline financial reconciliation and accounts payable processing. 

Each quarterly invoice aggregates all metered usage data, subscription tiers, and applicable adjustments accumulated across the preceding three-month billing cycle. Payment terms and remittance instructions are detailed directly on each statement. For any discrepancies or historical billing inquiries related to these quarterly statements, the financial operations team must be contacted through the designated enterprise support portal referencing the v1.0 billing agreement framework.
