For missed Service Level Agreement (SLA) targets, a credit schedule is applied to compensate Globex for the failure to meet the agreed-upon service standards. The credit schedule is based on the duration and severity of the SLA breach.

- A 1% credit is applied for each 15-minute interval of partial SLA breach (i.e., 0.25% for every 3 minutes of partial breach within the 15-minute interval).
- For full SLA breaches, a 2% credit is applied for each 15-minute interval.
- The maximum credit applied per month is 10% of the total invoice amount.
- Credits are calculated and applied to the next invoice, and are non-refundable.
- The credit schedule does not apply to planned maintenance or scheduled downtime.
