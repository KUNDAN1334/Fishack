The public link feature for dashboards was disabled across your Flowlytics workspace due to a compliance requirement. As an admin, you have the ability to configure workspace-wide settings, including those related to data sharing and accessibility. In this case, you temporarily disabled public links to ensure that sensitive data is not accessible to unauthorized parties. To re-enable public links, please navigate to the Workspace Settings > Security & Compliance section and toggle the 'Allow public links' setting to 'Enabled'. Once saved, public links will be available for your users to share dashboards with external parties.
