AWS PrivateLink is currently available for Globex deployments in the us-east and eu-west regions as of version v2.3. The absence of PrivateLink endpoints in other regions was due to isolated provisioning constraints on those specific clusters. To resolve this, upgrade your deployment to version v2.3 or later, which enables the native cross-region PrivateLink configuration utility. Once updated, you can configure your VPC endpoints directly through the Flowlytics infrastructure dashboard for us-east and eu-west. If you require PrivateLink connectivity in additional regions beyond us-east and eu-west, please open a separate feature request ticket so our infrastructure team can scope deployment timelines for your account.
