The retention period for raw events on the Growth plan is strictly 12 months. Any raw event data ingested prior to this 12-month window is automatically purged from our system as part of our standard data lifecycle policy. 

However, your historical metrics and long-term trends remain fully intact. While raw event logs are deleted after 12 months, all aggregated data is kept longer to ensure your reporting dashboards, billing metrics, and analytics trends are not disrupted. 

If you need access to raw event streams older than 12 months for compliance or custom auditing, we recommend exporting your data to a secure data warehouse using our automated pipeline before the 12-month retention threshold is reached.
