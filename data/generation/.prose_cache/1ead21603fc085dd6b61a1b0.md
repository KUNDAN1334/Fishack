The duplicate webhook deliveries you experienced were caused by a known race condition in our dispatch queue where retried events bypassed the deduplication check under high concurrency. This issue has been fully resolved in version 2.4 of the Flowlytics platform. 

If you are running an earlier version and cannot upgrade immediately, the interim workaround is to implement client-side deduplication by storing and checking the unique `event_id` property included in the webhook payload payload headers before processing. Please let us know if you observe any further anomalies after updating.
