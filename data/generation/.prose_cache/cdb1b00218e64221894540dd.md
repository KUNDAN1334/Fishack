Flowlytics v2.0 introduces general availability for the v2 API, featuring a redesigned event schema optimized for higher ingestion throughput and lower latency. 

Clients migrating from v1 must update their payload structures to comply with the updated schema definitions. Authentication mechanisms remain unchanged, but endpoint base paths now require the explicit `/v2/` prefix. Deprecated v1 endpoints will continue to receive critical bug fixes until the final sunset date.
