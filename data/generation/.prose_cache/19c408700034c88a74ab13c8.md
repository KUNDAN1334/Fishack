The silent failure of your Snowflake data sync was caused by a permissions regression on your data warehouse. Following a recent Snowflake-side change, the configured warehouse role lost USAGE privileges on the target schema, preventing Flowlytics from committing incremental analytics batches without throwing a blocking application error. 

To restore sync operations, execute the following SQL command in your Snowflake environment to re-grant the necessary permissions to your role: `GRANT USAGE ON SCHEMA target_schema TO ROLE flowlytics_role;`. Once applied, trigger a manual sync from your Flowlytics dashboard to confirm data ingestion has resumed normally.
