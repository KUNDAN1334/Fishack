The sync failure was caused by schema drift in your Snowflake destination. When columns are added, removed, or renamed downstream without updating the Flowlytics integration mapping, the ingestion pipeline halts to prevent data corruption. 

To resolve this issue, you need to re-run the setup step in your Flowlytics dashboard. Navigate to your data warehouse settings, select your Snowflake integration, and click re-configure. This prompts the platform to rescan the target schema and map the new columns. Once the mapping is saved, manually trigger a full sync to resume normal data flow.
