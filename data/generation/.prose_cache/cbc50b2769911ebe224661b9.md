Flowlytics v2.1 introduces cohort retention grids to track user engagement over specific time horizons. You can now analyze user retention patterns grouped by sign-up date, subscription tier, or custom event triggers. The grids display percentage-based retention rates across daily, weekly, and monthly intervals. This feature enables product and finance teams to identify churn inflection points, measure the long-term impact of onboarding changes, and correlate initial usage behavior with lifetime value more accurately.
