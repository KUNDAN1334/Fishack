Calls initiated from the Flowlytics dashboard, specifically identify and page calls, are considered billable events. This is due to the fact that these actions incur costs associated with the underlying infrastructure and services utilized by Flowlytics. On the other hand, internal system events, such as API calls and background tasks, are free of charge as they do not incur additional costs. As a result, customers are only charged for billable events, which are clearly outlined in their Flowlytics dashboard under the 'Billing' section. If you have any further questions or concerns regarding your billing, please don't hesitate to reach out to our support team.
