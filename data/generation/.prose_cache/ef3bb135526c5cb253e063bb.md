Globex operates on a quarterly commit structure designed to align billing with usage forecasts. Under this model, customers agree to a minimum expenditure threshold per quarter. 

If your actual usage falls below the agreed quarterly commit, you remain responsible for the full committed amount. Unused balances from one quarter do not roll over to subsequent quarters and are non-refundable. 

Conversely, any consumption exceeding the quarterly commit is billed immediately as overage at the standard rate outlined in your insertion order. 

Quarterly commitments reset on the first day of each applicable quarter at 00:00 UTC. To modify your commit level for future quarters, contact your account manager at least 30 days prior to the current quarter's end. All adjustments are subject to review and require a signed amendment to your existing agreement.
