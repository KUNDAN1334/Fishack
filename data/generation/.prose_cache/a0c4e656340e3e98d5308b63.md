Flowlytics v2.3 increases the maximum ingestion batch size to 500 events per request, doubling the previous limit of 250 events. This update reduces network overhead and improves throughput for high-volume data pipelines sending payloads to the ingestion endpoint. API clients submitting batches exceeding 500 events will continue to receive a validation error. Ensure your SDK configurations and local batching logic are updated to take advantage of this increased threshold.
