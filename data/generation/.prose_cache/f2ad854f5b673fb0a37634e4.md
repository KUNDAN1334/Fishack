The Chart Widget Catalog provides a comprehensive list of available chart types that can be added to dashboards in Flowlytics analytics v2.2. This catalog offers flexibility and customization options for users to effectively visualize their data.

The catalog includes the following chart types:
- Line Chart
- Bar Chart
- Pie Chart
- Scatter Plot
- Area Chart
- Column Chart

Each chart type can be further customized with various options, such as changing colors, adding labels, and adjusting data ranges. Users can also apply filters and drill-down capabilities to enhance data exploration and analysis. By utilizing the Chart Widget Catalog, users can create tailored dashboards that meet their specific needs and provide actionable insights into their business performance.
