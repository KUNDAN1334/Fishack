The internal sharing feature allows administrators to share dashboards with specific users or groups within their organization. This can be done by clicking the 'Share' button on the top right corner of the dashboard. A modal window will appear, prompting the administrator to select the desired sharing options.

Administrators can choose to share the dashboard with individual users or groups. The 'Viewer' role is available for internal sharing, which grants users read-only access to the dashboard. Users with the 'Viewer' role can view the dashboard but cannot edit or delete any content.

Public links can also be generated for dashboards, allowing external users to access the dashboard. However, public links do not support the 'Viewer' role and users will have full access to the dashboard. This option should be used with caution and only when necessary.
