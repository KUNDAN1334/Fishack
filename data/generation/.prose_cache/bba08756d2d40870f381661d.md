Prior to version 2.4, the Flowlytics SCIM integration supported user provisioning only, which explains why your group synchronization attempts previously failed. Group sync was officially shipped in version 2.4. 

To resolve the issue, please update your identity provider's SCIM configuration to point to our v2.4 endpoint and ensure that group attribute mapping is enabled in your IdP settings. Once configured, trigger a manual sync to populate your Flowlytics teams and permissions automatically. Let us know if you encounter any further errors during the sync process.
