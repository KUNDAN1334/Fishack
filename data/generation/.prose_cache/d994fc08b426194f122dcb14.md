Flowlytics webhook delivery uses an at-least-once delivery model with no ordering guarantee. Network partitions or downstream timeouts can cause retries to arrive out of sequence. 

To handle this correctly, your consumer service should not rely on the arrival sequence of HTTP requests. Instead, use the `timestamp` field included in the event payload to order events chronologically. Additionally, ensure your webhook handler implements idempotency using the unique `event_id` property. This prevents duplicate processing when retries occur after a temporary network failure or timeout on your endpoint.
