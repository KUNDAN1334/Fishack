# Acme Billing Terms


## Negotiated Rates

Flowlytics applies Acme Corporation's negotiated annual commit and custom rate card according to contract version v1.0. The annual commit is strictly enforced across all active workspaces and subscription tiers within the Acme organization. All usage exceeding the pre-paid annual commit volume is billed at the agreed-upon overage rates defined in the v1.0 rate card, without automatic tier upgrades. Unused commit amounts do not roll over to subsequent annual billing periods and are non-refundable. Platform administrators can monitor real-time consumption against the annual commit threshold directly from the billing overview dashboard. Any billing discrepancies related to the v1.0 rate card must be reported to Flowlytics support within thirty days of the invoice date.


## Purchase Order Process

Acme requires a valid Purchase Order (PO) number on every invoice generated through the Flowlytics platform. To ensure uninterrupted billing cycles and prevent payment processing delays, you must input your assigned PO number directly into your account settings prior to the start of each billing period. 

If a PO number is missing or fails validation against Acme's procurement system, the invoice will enter a pending state, and you will receive an automated notification detailing the discrepancy. You must update the invoice with the correct PO number within five business days to avoid suspension of platform services. For accounts operating under master services agreements with distinct invoicing workflows, please contact your designated account manager to configure automated PO ingestion rules.
