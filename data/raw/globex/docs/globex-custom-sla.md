# Globex Service Level Agreement


## Uptime Commitment

The Globex Service Level Agreement (SLA) includes a commitment to a minimum of 99.9% uptime. This commitment applies to the platform's core functionality and is calculated on a monthly basis. To determine uptime, Flowlytics monitors the platform's availability and calculates the percentage of time it is accessible to users. The uptime percentage is calculated using the following formula: (Total minutes in the month - Minutes of downtime) / Total minutes in the month * 100. For example, if the platform experiences 30 minutes of downtime in a 31-day month, the uptime would be 99.03%. Flowlytics will provide a notification to customers in the event of a planned or unplanned outage that results in downtime exceeding 30 minutes. In the event of a prolonged outage, customers may request a credit towards their monthly subscription.


## Support Response Times

The Globex Service Level Agreement (SLA) outlines the expected response times for critical issues. For Priority 1 (P1) incidents, Flowlytics guarantees a response within 2 hours. This ensures that severe problems affecting the core functionality of the platform are addressed promptly to minimize downtime and impact on business operations.

A P1 incident is defined as a critical issue that completely prevents users from accessing the platform or significantly impacts the ability to perform essential tasks. Examples of P1 incidents include, but are not limited to, complete data loss, platform unavailability, and severe performance degradation.

Flowlytics will respond to P1 incidents within 2 hours of receiving notification, and will work to resolve the issue as quickly as possible. If the issue cannot be resolved within 2 hours, a detailed update will be provided to the customer with an estimated time to resolution.


| Severity | Response | Resolution target |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | Standard | Standard |
| P2 | Extended | Extended |
| P3 | Custom | Custom |


## Service Credits

For missed Service Level Agreement (SLA) targets, a credit schedule is applied to compensate Globex for the failure to meet the agreed-upon service standards. The credit schedule is based on the duration and severity of the SLA breach.

- A 1% credit is applied for each 15-minute interval of partial SLA breach (i.e., 0.25% for every 3 minutes of partial breach within the 15-minute interval).
- For full SLA breaches, a 2% credit is applied for each 15-minute interval.
- The maximum credit applied per month is 10% of the total invoice amount.
- Credits are calculated and applied to the next invoice, and are non-refundable.
- The credit schedule does not apply to planned maintenance or scheduled downtime.
