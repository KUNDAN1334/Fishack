# Globex Billing Terms


## Negotiated Rates

Globex operates on a quarterly commit structure designed to align billing with usage forecasts. Under this model, customers agree to a minimum expenditure threshold per quarter. 

If your actual usage falls below the agreed quarterly commit, you remain responsible for the full committed amount. Unused balances from one quarter do not roll over to subsequent quarters and are non-refundable. 

Conversely, any consumption exceeding the quarterly commit is billed immediately as overage at the standard rate outlined in your insertion order. 

Quarterly commitments reset on the first day of each applicable quarter at 00:00 UTC. To modify your commit level for future quarters, contact your account manager at least 30 days prior to the current quarter's end. All adjustments are subject to review and require a signed amendment to your existing agreement.


## Consolidated Invoicing

Under the terms of the Globex billing agreement (version 1.0), Globex receives exactly one consolidated invoice per quarter for all platform usage and associated services managed through Flowlytics. This consolidated billing schedule replaces individual monthly statements to streamline financial reconciliation and accounts payable processing. 

Each quarterly invoice aggregates all metered usage data, subscription tiers, and applicable adjustments accumulated across the preceding three-month billing cycle. Payment terms and remittance instructions are detailed directly on each statement. For any discrepancies or historical billing inquiries related to these quarterly statements, the financial operations team must be contacted through the designated enterprise support portal referencing the v1.0 billing agreement framework.
